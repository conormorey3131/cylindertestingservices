Gas Cylinder Filling

Once a cylinder has passed inspection and testing, we can fill it on site — one visit, one invoice, one supplier for the full job.

Book a collection Call us

Tested and filled together

There's little point having a cylinder tested in one place and filled in another — it adds a step, a delay, and another supplier to manage. Because filling happens at the same facility as testing and refurbishment, your cylinders leave us tested, certified and ready for use.

This is particularly useful for gas and welding suppliers and beverage dispense customers who need cylinders back in rotation quickly. See gas & welding suppliers and brewing & beverage.

What we fill

Every cylinder type we test, we can fill — no restrictions on cylinder type or customer.

Industrial & welding gases

Oxygen, acetylene, argon, nitrogen, CO2 and shielding gas mixtures.

Beverage & dispense gases

CO2 and mixed gas for breweries, pubs and beverage suppliers.

Fire extinguisher & CO2

CO2 recharge for extinguisher servicing companies following overhaul.

Fire suppression agents

FM-200, Novec 1230, inert gas and CO2 suppression cylinders recharged to the correct fill weight.

Breathing air & dive

SCBA and scuba cylinders filled after test and inspection.

LPG

Propane and LPG cylinders for domestic and commercial use.
