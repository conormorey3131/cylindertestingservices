Nationwide Collection, Transport & Storage

Wherever you're based in Ireland, we can collect your cylinders, test and refurbish them, and get them back to you — without you having to organise your own transport.

Book a collection Call us
ADR road transport TPED compliant ISO 18119

Nationwide collection routes

We run collection routes across Ireland, so you're not left arranging courier quotes or driving cylinders to Kilmallock yourself. Tell us where you are and roughly how many cylinders you have, and we'll confirm the next available collection date for your area.

We are fully ADR compliant for the carriage of dangerous goods by road. Your cylinders travel on properly placarded vehicles, with trained drivers and the correct transport documentation, from the moment they leave your site until they're back on your rack.

Old or condemned cylinders can be collected on the same run for decommissioning and documented disposal.

Secure storage

If you need cylinders held before or after testing — for example, ahead of a delivery run or while you wait on stock levels — we can store them securely at our Kilmallock facility.

For customers who can't be without cylinders while theirs are being tested, we may be able to provide loan cylinders to keep you operating.VERIFY with client
