Cylinder Refurbishment & Painting

Shot blasting, repainting and valve servicing — done alongside testing so your cylinders go back out looking as good as they work.

Book a collection Call us

Why refurbish alongside testing

A cylinder that's due a periodic test is already stripped down and on our floor — the sensible time to deal with corrosion, chipped paint and a worn valve, rather than sending it back out to fail sooner than it should.

Refurbished cylinders are easier to read, easier to trust, and better represent your business when they're sitting in a customer's premises with your branding on them.

What refurbishment includes

Shot blasting

Removes old paint, rust and surface contamination back to bare metal, giving us — and you — a clear view of the cylinder's condition.

Painting

A fresh, durable coat finished to the correct colour coding for the gas the cylinder holds.

Valve servicing

Worn or damaged valves are serviced or replaced, so the cylinder is sound from the inside out, not just good-looking.

Fleet refurbishment

If you're managing a fleet of cylinders — fire extinguisher cylinders, dispense gas bottles, industrial gas stock — we can refurbish in batches to keep your fleet consistent and presentable, timed around your testing cycle.

Combine this with our nationwide collection and cylinders can be off your premises and back, refurbished and re-certified, with minimal disruption to your operation.
