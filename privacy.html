Privacy Notice

How we collect and use personal data through this website.

Who we are

This website is operated by Cylinder Testing Services, Railway Road, Kilmallock, Co. Limerick, V35 TH92, Ireland. For any privacy query, contact us at info@cylindertestingservices.ie or (063) 20700.

What we collect

When you submit our quote form, we collect the information you provide: name, company, phone number, email address, county, cylinder type, approximate quantity, whether you need collection, and any message you enter. We use this only to respond to your enquiry and, where you go ahead, to arrange testing, refurbishment, filling and collection of your cylinders.

How your form submission is handled

Our quote form is processed by a third-party form service (Web3Forms) which delivers your submission to us by email. Your data is transmitted to that provider in order to reach us and is not used by them for any purpose beyond delivering your enquiry. Website analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use this website — for example, which pages are viewed and how visitors arrive at the site. This script only runs if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner shown when you first visit. If you click "Decline", or take no action, analytics scripts are not loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser's cookies for this site, which will show the cookie banner again on your next visit.

We process quote form data on the basis of your consent and, where you proceed with our services, to take steps prior to entering a contract with you. We process analytics data on the basis of your consent, given via the cookie banner.
