When Is My Cylinder Due a Test?

Enter the cylinder type and the date of its last test, the one stamped on the neck or shoulder of the cylinder, and we'll work out when the next test typically falls due.

ISO 18119 TPED compliant ADR road transport

How to find the date

Cylinders are stamped, usually near the neck or shoulder, with a date mark showing when they were last tested, typically month and year, often alongside the test house's identification mark.

Find that date, pick the cylinder type, and the checker will add the typical interval for that type of cylinder and tell you whether the next test looks overdue, due within the next 12 months, or still in date.

If you're not sure how to read the marks on a particular cylinder, send us a photo of the neck stamps and we'll help. It's often cheaper to test and recertify a cylinder than to replace it.

A guide, not a guarantee

This checker is a guide based on typical intervals only. The exact interval for your cylinder depends on the cylinder itself, the gas it holds and the manufacturer's specification, and the stamp on the cylinder is what counts.

For a definite answer, send us a photo of the stamps on the cylinder neck or shoulder, or call us on (063) 69 698, and we'll confirm when your cylinder is next due.
