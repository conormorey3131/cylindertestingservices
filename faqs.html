Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers on cylinder testing. Where an answer depends on your specific cylinder or standard, we've said so — call us on (063) 20700 for a definite answer on your own fleet.

How often must a gas cylinder be tested?VERIFY

This depends on the cylinder type and the standard it's tested under. Industrial and fire extinguisher cylinders are typically due periodic inspection every few years. Check the date stamped on your cylinder, or send us the details and we'll confirm when it's next due. Exact intervals by cylinder type need to be verified against the applicable standard before we publish specific figures here.

How do I read the test date on my cylinder?

Cylinders are stamped, usually near the neck or shoulder, with a date mark showing when they were last tested — typically month and year, often alongside the test house's identification mark. If you're not sure how to read the marks on a particular cylinder, send us a photo and we'll help.

What happens during a periodic inspection?

An external examination of the cylinder shell, an internal examination once the valve is removed, a hydrostatic test to confirm the cylinder holds pressure safely, inspection of the valve and any pressure-relief device, and stamping and certification for cylinders that pass. Full detail on our cylinder testing page.

What is the Pi mark?VERIFY

The Pi mark shows a cylinder has been assessed and periodically inspected in line with EU transportable pressure equipment requirements, allowing it to be used and moved within the EU. Exact regulatory wording is being verified before this answer is finalised for publish.

My cylinders were tested in the UK — are they still valid here?VERIFY

It depends on which body carried out the test. Since Brexit, inspection by a body appointed under Great Britain's own regime is recognised for use in Great Britain, but typically doesn't carry EU (Pi-mark) validity on its own. This concerns Great Britain specifically — Northern Ireland sits under different arrangements. If you're not sure where your cylinders stand, send us the details and we'll advise. Final wording on this answer is to be verified with our notified body before publish. See also Why Test in Ireland.

Do you collect?

Yes. We run nationwide collection routes across Ireland. Tell us your county and roughly how many cylinders you have and we'll confirm the next available collection date. See collection & storage.

How long does it take?VERIFY

Typically days rather than weeks, though it depends on cylinder type, condition and how much refurbishment is needed. We'll confirm an estimated turnaround when you get in touch — exact typical turnaround times to be confirmed for this page.

What cylinder types do you test?

Fire extinguisher and CO2 cylinders, industrial and welding gas cylinders, beer gas and beverage dispense cylinders, dive cylinders, SCBA breathing-air cylinders, and LPG cylinders. Not sure about yours? Get in touch.

Do you supply loan cylinders?VERIFY

In some cases we may be able to provide loan cylinders while yours are being tested, so you're not left without stock. This needs to be confirmed with the client before we commit to it in writing here.

How much does it cost?VERIFY

Pricing depends on cylinder type, size, condition and quantity. Send us your details through our quote form or call (063) 20700 and we'll give you a price. We don't publish a fixed price list, as no two jobs are the same.

Do you refurbish and repaint?

Yes. Shot blasting, repainting and valve servicing are available alongside testing, so cylinders go back out looking as good as they work. See refurbishment.

Once a cylinder has passed inspection and testing, we can fill it on site, so it's ready to use when it leaves us. The specific gases we fill, and any restrictions, are to be confirmed — see cylinder filling.
