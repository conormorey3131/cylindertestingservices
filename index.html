Why test in Ireland?

Since Brexit, a periodic inspection carried out by a GB-appointed body doesn't automatically keep a cylinder's EU (Pi) validity for use here. Add the ferry, the customs paperwork and the downtime waiting on a UK turnaround, and shipping cylinders to Britain stops making sense.

Testing in Kilmallock means one Irish invoice, no customs, no ferry — and your cylinders back in days rather than weeks.
