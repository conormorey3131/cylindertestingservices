About Cylinder Testing Services

Ireland's only independent cylinder testing facility, based in Kilmallock, Co. Limerick.VERIFY

Our facility

We operate a dedicated cylinder testing and refurbishment facility on Railway Road in Kilmallock, Co. Limerick — hydrostatic test bays, shot blasting and painting equipment, and secure storage, all under one roof.

We test, refurbish and fill up to 1,000 cylinders a week for customers across Ireland, covering fire protection, gas and welding supply, brewing and beverage, diving, marine, and fire services and other public bodies.VERIFY

Part of the Stargas group

Cylinder Testing Services shares ownership with Stargas, an established industrial gas supplier. That relationship gives us a close, practical understanding of what gas suppliers, and the businesses that depend on them, actually need from a testing partner — reliable turnaround, straight answers, and cylinders you can trust back in the field.
