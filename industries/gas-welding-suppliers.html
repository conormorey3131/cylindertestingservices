Cylinder Testing for Gas & Welding Suppliers

Keep your rental fleet moving. Periodic testing, refurbishment and filling for industrial gas and welding cylinders, without the fleet sitting idle in transit to Britain.

Book a collection Call us

Fleet cylinders, tested on a schedule that suits you

Gas and welding suppliers run on rented cylinder fleets that need to keep moving between customers. Every cylinder off the road for testing is a cylinder not earning — and a UK round trip can take weeks once you account for freight and customs.

We test, refurbish and fill on site in Kilmallock, so cylinders due periodic inspection can be back in your yard and out to customers again in days.

What we handle

Oxygen & acetylene

Periodic inspection and hydrostatic testing of oxy-acetylene welding sets.

Argon, nitrogen & mixed gases

Testing of industrial gas cylinders used across manufacturing and engineering.

Refurbishment & refilling

Shot blasting, repainting and filling once testing is complete — see refurbishment and cylinder filling.
