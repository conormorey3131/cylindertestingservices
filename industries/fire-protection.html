Fire Extinguisher & CO2 Cylinder Testing

Periodic testing and CO2 overhaul for fire extinguisher and fire protection companies — plus the only fire suppression system cylinder service in Ireland. Done here, on a schedule that fits your servicing rounds.

Built around your service schedule

Extinguisher servicing companies work to IS 291 service schedules, with cylinders due periodic inspection at set intervals. When a batch comes due, sending them to Britain means freight, customs paperwork and weeks of downtime — cylinders that should be back on a customer's wall, sitting in transit instead.

We test and, where required, carry out CO2 overhaul work here in Kilmallock, so you can plan your servicing rounds around a predictable Irish turnaround rather than a UK courier schedule.

What we handle

CO2 extinguisher cylinders

Periodic inspection and hydrostatic testing, including CO2 overhaul where required.

Water & foam extinguisher cylinders

Testing and recertification for water and foam-based extinguisher bodies.

Fire suppression system cylinders

FM-200, Novec 1230, inert gas and CO2 suppression cylinders tested, serviced, refilled and decommissioned — the only facility in Ireland equipped to do it. Read more.

Refurbishment & repaint

Shot blasting and repainting so extinguishers look right on a customer's premises, not just test right. See refurbishment.

Fire suppression systems — unique in Ireland

Until now, cylinders from gaseous fire suppression systems — FM-200 (HFC-227ea), Novec 1230 (FK-5-1-12), inert gas systems such as IG-541, IG-55 and IG-100, and CO2 total-flooding systems — had to leave the country for periodic testing. We are the only facility in Ireland that is ISO 18119, TPED and ADR compliant and equipped to handle them.

  • Periodic inspection and hydrostatic testing of suppression system cylinders to ISO 18119 and TPED.
  • Valve and actuator servicing so the cylinder goes back into the system ready to operate.
  • Agent refilling — cylinders recharged with the correct suppression agent and weight after testing or a discharge.
  • ADR-compliant collection and return of high-pressure and agent-filled cylinders.
  • Decommissioning and documented disposal when a system is replaced or a building is stripped out — see cylinder disposal.
