Dive Cylinder Testing | Scuba & SCBA

Periodic testing for scuba dive cylinders and SCBA breathing-air cylinders, for dive centres, marine operators and fire services.

Book a collection Call us

Cylinders your divers depend on

A dive cylinder or breathing-air set is only as good as its last test. We carry out periodic inspection and hydrostatic testing to keep your fleet compliant and, more importantly, safe for the people using it.

Because we test in Kilmallock rather than shipping cylinders to Britain, you're not left short of stock during your busiest season.

Who this is for

Dive centres

Periodic testing of rental and personal scuba cylinders ahead of the diving season.

Marine operators

Testing of cylinders used in commercial marine and offshore work.

Fire & rescue services

SCBA breathing-air cylinder testing — see our public sector page.
